## 💡 About
FixSphere eliminates traditional offline friction in electronics repair. From missing paper bills to wasted hours in service center queues, FixSphere introduces client-side Neural OCR, a 10km GPS radar, and transparent doorstep dispatch.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🧠 Neural Vision Vault:** Snaps paper receipts and automatically extracts Brand, Model, IMEI/Serial, Date, and Price via client-side OCR (*Tesseract.js*).
* **🛰️ 10km GPS Radar:** Category-isolated routing engine that maps verified service hubs within a 10km radius (separating Smartphone vs. Laptop lounges).
* **🛵 Doorstep Dispatch:** Rapido-style on-demand technician booking with exact distance pricing (**₹49 Base + ₹15/km Fuel Charge**) and confirmation calls.
* **🔐 Cryptographic Receipts:** Generates downloadable digital thermal PNG receipts to prevent warranty claim rejections from lost paper bills.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend & 3D Shaders:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JS (3D Perspective Tilt)
- **Geospatial Radar:** Leaflet.js, Geoapify Geocoding API, Haversine Formula
- **Neural OCR:** Tesseract.js (Client-Side Edge Inference)
- **Deployment:** Netlify Edge CDN (Sub-second Global Load)
